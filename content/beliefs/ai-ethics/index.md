### I am against the wholesale firing of developers in a shortsided move by companies to reduce costs

### I stand for individual privacy rights, and all people should have to actively opt-in to any system that uses them as training data

### I stand for the rights of those protesting against AI data center development

### I am against the fly-by-night replacement of existing, working systems with experimental AI products

### I believe all AI generated content must be clearly and explicitly labelled in all contexts

### I believe that a developer who uses AI generated code is directly, completely, and unavoidably culpable for all effects of that code
